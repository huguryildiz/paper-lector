# Evidence record

Use the fields the task needs; leave no empty sections. Save it only when the user or project asks.

## Claim

Claim id and version; the question or decision; system, units, information timing, scope and assumptions; authorizations and limits; outcomes already seen.

## Sources

| Source, version, locator | What it establishes | Reading level | Relationship to this claim | SHA-256 of the PDF read | Decisive quote with page | Retrieved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

The last three columns are for a frozen novelty check; leave them out elsewhere. The hash applies only when a PDF was read.

Evidence types stay distinct: sourced, assumed, derived, computed, estimated, hypothesized, unknown. An inaccessible paper is an unresolved lead, not "no overlap".

## Search

Tool, mode, exact query, date, filters, cap, returned count, deduplication, screening decisions, unresolved leads, inaccessible close papers.

## Candidates

Stable ids, rejected alternatives with a reason and a revisit trigger. A reframed claim gets a new record and inherits no verdict.

## Test

Comparison contract, the simple explanation, why the control discriminates, inputs and outputs with units, validation, outcome meanings, and whether each was fixed before or after seeing results.

## Result

Observed values, uncertainty or exact guarantee and its scope, which explanation survives, what changed, what remains unknown, reviewer models and whether they were independent, and one next action. Keep superseded claims and negative results traceable.
